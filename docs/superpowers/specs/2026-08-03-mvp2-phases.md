## Principle

Section order in the PRD is **not** a work order. Phases are sequenced by
dependency and by the rule: **after Phase 3 the product works** — a person
can stay signed in, record money via the bot (including transfers/exchange),
and see the result on Home in the mini app.

Voice (PRD §9) and receipt photo (PRD §10) are **not launch conditions**.
Receipt photo is the **last** phase and must stay maximally isolated so a
go/no-go after the customer's 20-receipt test does not break anything else.

## Why this order (decisions inside AGENTS.md / PRD)

- **§8 before Home.** A bot that cannot parse transfers silently destroys money
  (PRD §8.1). Correctness of quick entry lands before polishing Home.
- **Home before History/Analytics/Settings.** After Phase 3 the family can
  already see what the bot wrote — earliest workable product.
- **Personal wallets after shared Settings entities.** Creating personal
  wallets needs the wallets settings surface; `Мои личные` extends Home.
- **Members after personal wallets.** Join-with-budget conversion turns old
  wallets into personal (§13.2); personal model must exist first.
- **Change log after History forms.** Editing others needs History edit path.
- **Notifications after Goals.** Digest goal line and achievement message
  depend on Goals.
- **Bot chrome late.** `/start` and release announcement are launch packaging,
  not a capability gap for the working path.
- **Prompt caching after parsing is stable.** Cost, not a user capability;
  may be pulled forward after Phase 2 without renumbering product phases.
- **Voice before receipt photo; receipt photo last.** Both post-launch; photo
  is cancelable without breaking earlier phases.

## Cross-cutting rules for every phase

- User-facing Russian strings: character-for-character from the PRD.
- Banned words in user-facing text: ошибка, сессия, сервер, токен, запрос.
- No automatic currency conversion anywhere.
- Design file `docs/design/Chontak MVP2.dc.html` is mandatory for mini-app
  screens; bot has no design file.
- If PRD and design disagree: stop and ask (except approved §17.4 UZS/USD on
  Графики).
- Workers: `composer-2.5` only. Orchestrator: Cursor Grok 4.5.
- MVP 1 code may be reused where behaviour still matches; do not assume it
  matches MVP 2 — verify against this PRD.
- Parsing / speech env vars (customer fills keys): `PARSER_PROVIDER`,
  `PARSER_API_KEY`, `PARSER_MODEL`, `SPEECH_PROVIDER`, `SPEECH_API_KEY`,
  `SPEECH_MODEL`. Model names come **only** from the environment — never
  hard-coded. Providers: Google (customer decision).
